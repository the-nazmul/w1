Nazmul Ahasan is Executive Editor at Netra News, a public-interest journalism platform in Bangladesh. Previously he was a US economy reporter at Bloomberg News in Washington, DC.

Earlier he was an editor at Radio Free Asia's BenarNews, shaping coverage across South and Southeast Asia.

He graduated from the UC Berkeley Graduate School of Journalism in 2023. His work has appeared in Foreign Policy, The Economist, The Telegraph, Haaretz, Columbia Journalism Review, and the San Francisco Chronicle. Before moving to the United States in 2021, he worked at The Daily Star and Netra News in Dhaka.
